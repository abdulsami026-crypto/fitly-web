Privacy

Your files

Photos, images and text you use in Fitly are processed inside your browser. They are not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see them.

What the website collects

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested and your IP address, for security and to keep the site running. Fitly itself has no accounts and asks for no personal details.

Ads and analytics

If we add advertising or analytics in the future, they will be provided by third parties who may use cookies, and this page will be updated first. They will never receive the files you process.
