Convert HEIC to JPG

iPhone photos come as HEIC, and many forms and devices cannot open them. Drop them here and get JPG or PNG back — in your browser, nothing uploaded.

Drop HEIC photos here or tap to choose .heic or .heif files, straight from an iPhone. Add as many as you like.

The HEIC engine downloads once the first time you convert, then it works offline.

How to use it

  1. Choose the format you want to convert to.
  2. Drop your images in the box, then press Convert.
  3. Download each file, or all of them as a ZIP.

Questions

Which format should I choose?

JPG suits photos and works everywhere. PNG keeps sharp edges and transparency. WebP gives smaller files for the web.

What happens to transparent areas?

PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPG cannot, so transparent areas turn white.

Does converting to PNG reduce the file size?

Usually not. PNG is lossless, so photos often get larger as PNG. Use JPG or WebP for smaller files.
