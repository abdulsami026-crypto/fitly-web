Passport photo maker

Turn any photo into a print-ready ID photo at the exact size — framed by you, sized by Fitly, and never uploaded.

Drop a photo here or tap to choose JPG, PNG or WebP. One photo, taken against a plain wall works best.

Your photo will appear here with a frame and a face guide. Drag it into place, zoom if you need to, then download.

Photos are made at 300 DPI, the standard for printing. Print sheets include cut guides.

How to use it

  1. Add a photo — a plain, light wall behind you gives the cleanest result.
  2. Pick the size your form or embassy asks for.
  3. Drag the photo so your face sits inside the dashed oval, and zoom until your head and shoulders fill it the way the guide shows.
  4. Download the photo for online forms, or a print sheet for paper copies.

Questions

What size will my photo be?

Exactly the size you pick, at 300 DPI: 35 × 45 mm is 413 × 531 pixels, 2 × 2 inch is 600 × 600 pixels, 33 × 48 mm is 390 × 567 pixels and 40 × 60 mm is 472 × 709 pixels.

How do I print the photo?

Download a print sheet: the 4 × 6 inch sheet fits standard photo paper, the A4 sheet fits a home printer. Print at 100% size — do not choose "fit to page" — and cut along the dashed lines.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is framed and sized inside your browser on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, which is also why the tool works offline after your first visit.

Can I change the background colour?

Yes. Pick a background colour and Fitly separates you from the background on your device, then fills it with the colour you chose — white for most forms, or any colour you like. The background tool downloads once the first time you use it, then it works offline like everything else here.

What makes a good source photo?

Stand against a plain, light-coloured wall in even light, face the camera, and leave space around your head and shoulders. The background tool handles most backgrounds, but a plain wall still gives the cleanest edges.
