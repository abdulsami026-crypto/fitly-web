How to use it

  1. Merge: add two or more PDFs, order them with Up and Down, then tap "Merge into one PDF".
  2. Split: add one PDF, type the pages you want (like 1-3, 5), and extract them into a new PDF — or split every page into its own file, zipped together.
  3. Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded, so the tool also works offline after your first visit.

Questions

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. Merging and splitting happen inside your browser on your own device. Your files never leave your phone or computer.

How do I write page ranges?

Use page numbers starting at 1. A single page is just its number (5), a run of pages is first-last (1-3), and you can mix them with commas: 1-3, 5, 8-10.

Can I change the page order before merging?

Yes. Add your PDFs, then use the Up and Down buttons on each file to set the order. The merged PDF follows that order exactly.

What if my PDF is password-protected?

Locked PDFs can't be opened in the browser. Remove the password first (in your PDF reader's print-to-PDF, for example), then merge or split the unlocked copy.

Is there a size limit?

No fixed limit. Very large files just take longer, especially on a phone — everything still happens on your device.
