Resize a photo under 1 MB without cropping

Add your photos and Fitly shrinks each one below your size limit. The whole picture is kept, only the file size changes.

Drop photos here or tap to choose JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. Add as many as you like.

Nothing is ever cropped. By default Fitly lowers quality first and reduces the pixel dimensions only if it has to. Choose "Always keep original width and height" to keep every pixel of the frame and let quality drop instead. Transparent PNGs get a white background when saved as JPG; choose WebP to keep transparency.

How to use it

  1. Drop your photos in the box, or tap it to choose them.
  2. Set the size limit. The default is 1000 KB, just under 1 MB.
  3. Download one photo, or use Download all to get a ZIP file.

Questions

Will my photo be cropped?

No. Fitly never crops. If a photo cannot reach the limit with quality changes alone, it reduces the width and height slightly and keeps the same proportions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The resizing happens inside your browser on your own device.

Can I resize many photos at once?

Yes. Add as many as you like and download them together as a ZIP file.

Should I pick JPG or WebP?

JPG opens everywhere and is the safe choice for forms. WebP makes smaller files at the same quality but some older sites do not accept it.
