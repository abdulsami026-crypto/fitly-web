Drop photos here or tap to choose JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. Add as many as you like.

Nothing is ever cropped. By default Fitly lowers quality first and reduces the pixel dimensions only if it has to. Choose "Always keep original width and height" to keep every pixel of the frame and let quality drop instead. Transparent PNGs get a white background when saved as JPG; choose WebP to keep transparency.
