Most file tools make their money from your files — uploads, accounts, watermarks. Fitly was built the other way around.

Questions

Is Fitly really free?

Yes. Every tool and every feature is free, with no sign-up and no watermark. There is no paid version waiting to unlock your own file.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere. Fitly has no server for your files to go to. Everything is processed by your own browser, on your own device, which is also why it works offline.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — Fitly is built phone-first, because most people meet a size limit while filling a form on their phone. It works the same on a computer.

Will a resized photo still look sharp?

Fitly lowers quality gently before it ever touches dimensions, and stops the moment the file fits. At the size forms display photos, the result looks the same.

Which formats can I use?

JPG, PNG and WebP for images. The PDF tool turns images into PDF documents, and the QR generator gives you PNG or SVG codes.
