Drop photos here or tap to choose JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP. Add as many as you like.

Nothing is ever cropped. By default Fitly lowers quality first and reduces the pixel dimensions only if it has to. Choose "Always keep original width and height" to keep every pixel of the frame and let quality drop instead. Transparent PNGs get a white background when saved as JPG; choose WebP to keep transparency.

A 1 MB limit is tiny. Fitly will reduce the pixel dimensions as well as the quality, so expect a small image. That is usually fine for signatures, stamps and small ID photos.

How to use it

  1. Drop your photo in the box, or tap it to choose it.
  2. The limit is already set to 1 MB. Change it if your form asks for something different.
  3. Check the result, then download it.

Questions

Will the photo still look good at 1 MB?

It will be smaller than the original, so some detail is lost. Fitly keeps as much quality as the limit allows.

Does it crop the image?

No. The whole image is kept. If needed, the width and height are reduced with the same proportions.

What if my form asks for exact dimensions?

Use the longest side box to set a maximum width or height. If you need exact pixel dimensions or a different shape, crop the photo first, then bring it here.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
